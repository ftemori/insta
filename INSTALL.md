# Installing Insta

Download files from the [latest release](../../releases/latest).

## Android

1. On your phone, open the latest release and download **Insta.apk**.
2. Tap the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to "install unknown apps".
3. Open Insta and log in with your Instagram account.

Updates: download the new Insta.apk and install it over the old one. You stay logged in.

## iPhone (free, no Mac)

Apple only lets apps signed with a free Apple ID run for **7 days** before they need a refresh. SideStore does the refresh on your phone. You need a Windows PC for the first setup only.

### One-time setup (about 20 minutes)

1. On your PC, install **iTunes** and **iCloud** from Apple's website (the versions from apple.com, not the Microsoft Store), then install **[Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io/)**.
2. Download **Insta.ipa** from the latest release to your PC.
3. Connect your iPhone with a USB cable. Unlock it and tap **Trust**.
4. Open Sideloadly, drag in Insta.ipa, enter your Apple ID and click **Start**.
   You can use a spare Apple ID if you prefer. Sideloadly signs the app with it.
5. On the iPhone:
   - **Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode**: turn on and restart.
   - **Settings > General > VPN & Device Management**: tap your Apple ID and **Trust**.
6. Open Insta and log in with your Instagram account.

### Refreshing every 7 days

**Option 1 (easiest):** plug into your PC once a week and run Sideloadly again with the same Insta.ipa. Your login stays.

**Option 2 (no PC after setup):** install **[SideStore](https://sidestore.io/)** by following its guide, then add Insta.ipa in SideStore. SideStore refreshes apps on the phone over Wi-Fi. Open it once a week and tap **Refresh All**.

If the 7 days run out, Insta just won't open until you refresh. Nothing is lost.

### Updates

Download the new Insta.ipa and install it the same way. It replaces the old one.

## Try it in a browser first (optional)

`insta.user.js` in the release is the same hiding script as a userscript:

- **iPhone Safari**: install the free **Userscripts** app from the App Store, enable it in Safari extensions, and add the file.
- **Desktop / Android Firefox**: install the Violentmonkey add-on and open the file.

Then visit instagram.com.
